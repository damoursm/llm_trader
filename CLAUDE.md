## Architecture

### Pipeline flow (`src/pipeline.py`, `run_pipeline()`)

```
0.  Universe        — (SHADOW) static watchlist + discovery; the discovery gate drops junk, exotic types and names
                      below the OBSERVATION floor ($1 / $1M).
1.  News            — (SHADOW) every feed → the merged, archived article pool → per-ticker digests scored by the
                      local Qwen.
2.  Snapshots       — Polygon batch (live + NBBO) → yfinance → grouped-daily close.
3.  Context, scores — (SHADOW) smart money, macro, regime & timing, the method scorers in [-1, +1].
4.  Aggregation     — (SHADOW) the ML-stacker combine → TickerSignal.
5.  Recommendations — (SHADOW) the rank rule: computed, gated, persisted, never opened.
5S. THE LIVE BOOKS  — the selection short, the dip long, the crash-rebound and the capitulation books. The scorer
                      subprocess and the
                      borrow-file fetch start at tick start and the WHOLE live path runs FIRST, before Step 0: the
                      marks, every live book's exits, the live account's check, the dip fills and the crash buy, the
                      dip orders, the shorts' entries (after the scorer's live arms) and a broker sync, then THE
                      SECOND ACCOUNT's process (its own ledger on these decisions, `src/accounts/`); Step 0
                      pins the trades open BEFORE those entries (the tick's new shorts never join its shadow
                      universe). A slot due while the shadow steps below still run gets its own live path on time
                      (`run_live_path`, its broker problems logged CRITICAL).
6.  Tracking        — the ledger marks, the live books' steps again, the (empty) legacy exit stack, the broker sync.
7.  Charts + email.
```

**Liquidity.** Each live book applies its own floors (the shorts: price ≥ $5 at the bar and a 20-session mean RTH dollar volume ≥ $5M from the deep store; the dip book: ≥ $1B a day; the crash book trades QQQ). - **Per-trade return** (`tracker._pct_return`): cost-aware buy-and-hold (`cost_in` / `cost_out` = half-spread + commission; SELL mirrors BUY); open trades are marked live.
- **Costs per leg** (`spread.resolve_leg_cost`, `sim_per_trade_cost_attribution`): the leg's own fill → its OWN QUOTE at submit (from the 2026-10-19 session, AUDIT151, the user's go-ahead: the broker stamps the book it priced the order on, `broker_bid_at_submit` / `broker_ask_at_submit` and the exit's; `spread.quote_leg_cost_frac`: the quoted half-spread, at most 400 bp, + IBKR's commission on the leg's shares + on a sale the SEC fee and FINRA's TAF — the buckets below were calibrated on calm names' fills, ~23 bp all-in, where the arms' volatile names quote 50–80 bp) → the same-tick average of fills in its liquidity class → the tick average → the time-of-day period average in its class (shrunk) → the period average → `_one_side_cost` (the calibrated real-fill overrides; the hand-built spread formula only on a fills-free database or for sub-minimum-price legs). Commission is a fee CEILING (`ibkr_fixed` max($1, $0.005/share), ×1.5). `calibrate_sim_costs` runs before each tick's mark and entry steps. Tests: `tests/test_cost_buckets.py`.
- **Short borrow carry** (`enable_short_borrow_cost`): every stock short pays IBKR's fee DAY BY DAY (user directive 2026-10-06: "we want the real exact borrow fees we would have in live trading a real account and we want that data to be as complete as possible"; `src/performance/borrow_fees.py`, `enable_ibkr_borrow_schedule`): every calendar day from the short's settlement to the cover's — IBKR's trade date (an overnight-session execution belongs to the next session), T+1 on days that settle (Columbus and Veterans Day trade but never settle) — shares × roundup(1.02 × the prior session's close, to the dollar) × THAT day's rate / 360. Each day, in order: what IBKR charged our account (`broker_borrow_fees`, per share); IBKR's own rate with the formula — our archive's last file of the day, IBKR's API bar, the community archive (`borrow_history.fee_daily`), carried over a day none holds for at most a week (or from around the entry), the file in force for a day not over (provisional); the entry stamp; `short_borrow_annual_pct` 3%. Stored on the trade (`borrow_days` [day, source, rate, collateral price, fee per share], `borrow_fee_ps` / `borrow_fee_usd`, `borrow_rate_now`, `borrow_final`) and charged by `_pct_return`, the daily NAV (each day in its interval) and so the simulated account. A short CLOSED before the schedule existed keeps that flat carry until `borrow_backfill_closed` — the user's condition: the formula backfills only once checked against IBKR's own charges (`python -m src.performance.borrow_fees --verify`). No look-ahead: a day not over reads only the file in force and closes final from 16:15 ET (`tests/test_no_lookahead.py`). Tests: `tests/test_borrow_fees.py`.
- **Dividends** (from the 2026-10-19 session, AUDIT151, the user's go-ahead; `src/performance/dividends.py`, `enable_ledger_dividends`): a live book's long RECEIVES and its short PAYS (the payment in lieu) every ex-date it held through — opened before 20:00 ET of the session before the ex-date and still held then (IBKR dates a later execution to the next session) — Polygon's USD cash (the deep store's `dividends` family, refreshed nightly; summed per ex-date) brought into the trade's terms by the splits executed after the ex-date, FROZEN on the trade when first due (``dividends``: ex-date, cash, ``frac`` of the entry price) at a mark or a close, never before its instant (`tests/test_no_lookahead.py`); `_pct_return(dividend=)` adds it to a long and takes it from a short, the daily NAV books it on the ex-date's interval, the live account follows through the return; a record that reaches the store after a close within 7 days is still added (`finalize_closed`). Tests: `tests/test_ledger_dividends.py`.
- **IBKR borrow book** (`src/data/ibkr_borrow.py`): IBKR's public short-stock file (`ftp2.interactivebrokers.com`, user `shortstock`; shares available + annual fee for ~19,750 symbols) is downloaded on its own thread from each tick's start (`pipeline._start_borrow_snapshot`, `enable_ibkr_borrow_snapshot`; `ibkr_borrow_hosts` falls back to ftp3) and archived RAW in `data/ibkr_borrow/<ET date>/<HHMMSS>.txt.gz` (never delete — IBKR keeps no history); `borrow_at(ticker, when)` reads the file in force at any instant. Every NEW equity short is checked when opened (`enable_short_borrow_gate`): the selection short needs ≥ $10k available at any fee. No snapshot within 120 min → a new short WAITS for one (`ibkr_borrow_require_file`; off: not checked, warned); futures / indices / FX / crypto are never judged. Tests: `tests/test_ibkr_borrow.py`.
- **Daily NAV** (`daily_nav.compute_compound_return`): a path-faithful daily walk over cached closes, capital-weighted per day and compounded; deterministic given the database.
- **Splits during a hold** (`tracker.apply_splits`, `enable_split_adjustment`; PREREG64, user 2026-10-09: "Fix the bugs in production"): from 04:00 ET of a split's execution date — the pre-market open; the overnight hours before it still quote the old terms — every OPEN trade is rescaled through the splits executed since its entry (the split data `intraday_store.split_rows`: Polygon lists announced splits ahead, refreshed nightly), as IBKR rescales the position: the price fields (`SPLIT_PRICE_FIELDS`: entry, reference close, fill, target, the pick's closes, the dip's close / trend / limit / ATR — never the crash long's close / trend, its decision symbol QQQ's) x the factor, the share counts (`SPLIT_SHARE_FIELDS`: the accounts' shares, the broker's requested and filled quantities, the dip order's quantity and fill cap) / it — exact, a reverse split's fraction kept (IBKR pays it in cash; the broker's counts truncate); the dollars, the return and the cost fractions unchanged. Applied at every ledger load (`_load_trades`) and the broker's own (`reconcile.sync`), once (`split_factor`, the values before it in `split_orig`, `split_log`); the current mark carried from the terms of its own time; a fill IBKR reports later for an order submitted before the split (`reconcile._split_k`) and IBKR's borrow charges of earlier days (per share of THEIR day) come into the same terms; the squeeze cover reads the original entry times the factor (never twice). Without it a 1-for-10 reverse split read as a 900% loss on a short — a false margin call of the whole account — and the broker's share counts no longer matched IBKR's. Tests `tests/test_splits.py`. THE SPLITS ARE VERIFIED (user 2026-10-16: "implement automated verification or testing and guardrails to prevent error in the data on the future (i.e. wrong or missing splits)"; `src/data/split_check.py`): every reader of the split data — the ledger, the broker, the stores' resets, the turn, the cover, the dip orders, the borrow schedule — gets the EFFECTIVE set (`intraday_store.split_rows`; Polygon's records as recorded: `raw_split_rows`): the records MINUS those the traded prices contradict (no move like the split's factor on the raw, unadjusted prices of its date and of the 5 sessions after it, AND no split in yfinance's list: `false` — XRX's "2-for-3" of 2026-02-09 never happened, yet Polygon's adjusted bars scaled its history by 2/3), in `cache/ml/deep/split_checks.json`; the MISSING splits yfinance confirms (a session jump of a standard split ratio in production's store that no record explains: `missing`) correct the price stores only, never the ledger — such a jump can be a spin-off (POST 2022-03-11, BellRing), which leaves IBKR's share count unchanged; an unreachable second source, or sessions after its date not all traded yet (a record dated a day early shows its move then; yfinance lists a split once it has traded), keeps a contradicted record applied (`suspect`, alerted, re-checked), and a past record with no session after it is `unverifiable`, never left pending. Every price store applies the table's CORRECTIONS to what it fetches from Polygon (`split_check.correct_frame`: a false record's adjustment undone, a missing split's applied); a `suspect` record's split is not loaded into production's store (`reset_split_tickers`: the history keeps its terms until the record is decided). THE LIVE GUARD: at the marks (`update_open_trades`), from 04:00 ET of a split's date (its effect: the overnight ticks before it quote the old terms), a held name's undecided split of the last 3 days is checked on Polygon's RAW prices of its date (`split_check.verify_held`, never a mark — a stale or already-converted mark proves nothing): verified, or contradicted -> `suspect` (CRITICAL log, scorer banner), STILL APPLIED — the guard never drops a record (a real split judged too early, or dated a day early, would leave a held short unrescaled: a fake 900% loss); it leaves the effective set once the nightly check calls it false, and the next ledger load restores the trade (`apply_splits`). Tests `tests/test_split_check.py`, `tests/test_integrity.py`.
- **Win rates are GROSS** (standing directive): a win is the right direction on raw prices (`tracker.gross_return_pct`, `is_gross_win`, `gross_win_rate`); costs live in the RETURN metrics only. Pinned by `tests/test_gross_winrate_convention.py`.

### Adding a live book (an ARM)

The combination grows by arms (Standing rules: THE COMBINATION'S DESIGN); the crash-rebound book is the template (`src/signals/crash_long.py`, `docs/CRASH_LONG.md`). The touchpoints:

1. **Signal module** `src/signals/<book>.py` with its `MECHANISM`, its decision journals under `cache/ml/<book>/` and a `health()` whose problems ride the scorer banner (`pipeline`, beside `crash_long.health`); settings `enable_<book>` + `<book>_*` in `config/settings.py`, every one read (`tests/test_inert_settings.py`).
2. **A live book** in `src/performance/books.py` (`LIVE_MECHANISMS`): without it the legacy exit stack and signal reversal treat its trades as legacy and the broker never sends its unfilled entries (`LEGACY_ENTRY_SHADOWED`).
3. **The live account** (`src/performance/live_account.py`): its positions in `state` and `margin_call` and in IBKR's liquidation (`funded_open`, `shares_of`, `maint_of` / `regt_of`; its share and notional fields in `tracker._ACCOUNT_SHARE_KEYS` / `_ACCOUNT_NOTIONAL_KEYS`, which a lot split divides), a `size_*`, a give-way plan if it may take the dip book's room; its trades stamp `<book>_account_shares`, which `reconcile._entry_qty` orders exactly.
4. **The ledger steps** in `tracker.py` (record / monitor, its margin-call close in `monitor_live_account`) and their calls in BOTH places in `pipeline.py` — the live path's first half (`_live_marks_and_exits`) and the end-of-tick block.
5. **Splits**: its price and share fields in `tracker.SPLIT_PRICE_FIELDS` / `SPLIT_SHARE_FIELDS`.
6. **The simulator**: an adapter in `src/backtest/strategies/` built on the production code, its `BOOK` / `PRIORITY` / `QUALITY` in `src/backtest/mimic.py` and a `Strategy` in `live_strategies` behind a `Mimic` flag (`Mimic.off()` without it).
7. **Tests**: its own file, a `tests/test_no_lookahead.py` guard checked to FAIL against an injected leak, the account's tests.
8. **Docs**: a runbook in `docs/`, its section here, `docs/BACKTEST.md`.
9. **Its card and its place in the bank**: a `reliability.SPEC` entry (mechanism, context, horizon — naming its settings — and its parameters, switches and sizing: the arm's fingerprint), `reliability.ARMS` / `SIDE` / `INIT_RATE`, `bank.ARMS` / `live_slices`, its card built on every pooled set (`--build` for A and B, `--build --sets A0`) and its qualification read (`python -m src.backtest.bank`, THE EVALUATION STANDARD; a long arm's bar in `bank.BENCH` — `tests/test_bank.py` pins one for every long arm of `reliability.SIDE`) before the deploy request; the live calibration's `arm_of` maps its trades to the arm.

### Broker integration

`src/broker/` is the real execution leg beside the internal ledger ("shadow & reconcile"). Modes `off | dry_run | ibkr_paper | ibkr_live` (`broker_mode`, live: `ibkr_paper`, port 4002; the factory refuses paper on a known live port). Interactive Brokers through `ib_async` (imported lazily) → IB Gateway under IBC (`docs/DEPLOYMENT.md`).

- **`reconcile.sync()`** runs TWICE per tick — in the live path right after the selection short's entries (the report persisted at once), and at the end of the tick (a late scorer's picks, a same-tick resend of anything the first left unfilled; the two reports are merged) — idempotent: **(0)** fill refresh by `orderRef`; the buy-in check and the orphan sweep (below); **(0b)** tick-scoped order lifetime — unfilled orders are cancelled and their legs cleared, EXCEPT an order of an earlier tick still working within `broker_rest_max_drift_bps` (60) of its decision price, which rests up to `broker_unfilled_cancel_minutes` (90) (`broker_rest_unfilled_orders`; a Rule 201 short therefore can rest past the next tick); the dip book's resting orders are synced next (`_sync_dip_orders`); the live books' entries (`books.is_live`) are re-anchored and resent while their row is open (the price-aware gate, `broker_resubmit_max_adverse_bps`, is for legacy entries, and an unfilled legacy entry is never sent: `LEGACY_ENTRY_SHADOWED`); exits are never gated; **(0c)** cancel-on-close; **(1)** entries size the GAP against any same-side holding NO OTHER open trade owns (`_owned_by_open`: the selection short stacks several open trades on one name, 2026-09-28); **(2)** closed trades still holding a position are closed from the actual position MINUS what open trades in the ticker own and MINUS the shares already being covered (the unfilled rest of working covers, covers sent earlier in the pass) — two closed trades on one name never cover it twice; a dead entry leg stays parked only when shares no other open trade owns back it; a closed trade whose own entry never filled sends nothing (`NEVER_FILLED`, terminal) and no position row means flat (no order) — never an exit sized from the recorded fill; an order IBKR REFUSES outright is resent in the same tick (`broker_refused_resends_per_tick`) and parked for the next (an entry is given up after `broker_refused_max_ticks`, an exit never); **(3)** drift → `broker_drift_action="flatten"` (never adopted into the ledger; `ibkr_live` always refuses auto-flatten); **(4)** settle — fill fast or kill (`broker_settle_seconds` 30, re-anchored every ~6 s).
- **Invariant**: no order works the book unless a ledger leg points at it — `_persist_legs` flushes after every submission, `cancel_order` cancels every order with a ref, a pre-submit duplicate guard, an orphan sweep, `reqAllOpenOrders()` on connect. ## Standing rules

- **THE EVALUATION STANDARD — ONE POOLED HISTORY, A BANK OF MODELS** (user directive 2026-10-14: "Instead of checking for the three different sets (A0, A, and B), check for the full dataset (A0+A+B). Each individual model needs to give growth on that whole set but doesn't need to have growth all the time. I.e., a model could be good only under certain market conditions that you will need to find. So, some of the models might need more filters or ways to adapt to the market. What's important is that over the years 2018-2026 it has performed well when it was activated and didn't produce a drawback that was too high. This means that winrate and return per day are important metrics. In an effort to not overtfit, we also need to have a enough trades made so that the results are significant, reliable, and transferable to out of sample data. To make sure the combination algorithm still has good yearly and monthly growth, we then need multiple models in our bank of models so that we cover all market conditions."). THE POOLED HISTORY = sets A0 + A + B (`periods.POOLED`), decisions 2018-01-02 – 2026-09-24: every decision about a model or the combination reads it WHOLE; the sets stay the simulator's construction (each its own borrow data, Evaluation: THE TEST SETS) and are reported beside the calendar years and the market contexts, never decided on alone; until one continuous run spans them (PREREG105's rebuilt stores) the pooled numbers chain the sets' accounts — a pooled rate is the years-weighted mean of their log rates (`periods.pool_weights`), a pooled drawdown the worst of theirs. `python -m src.backtest.bank` → `cache/ml/bank/<day>.json` applies it mechanically (`src/backtest/bank.py`, `tests/test_bank.py`). (1) A MODEL — an arm — belongs in THE BANK when, on the pooled history, its own trades (the reliability cards: each arm alone at the live costs) show ENOUGH TRADES — at least 100 in at least 30 distinct entry months (a month's trades share its market: the months are its independent episodes) —, SIGNIFICANCE — the 95% interval of its RETURN PER DAY (a bootstrap over entry months) above its BAR —, TRANSFERABILITY — its return per day above its bar in both chronological halves of its trades and with ANY one calendar year left out — and GROWTH — the arm alone at its live slice of a $10,000 account grows over the whole history; beside them its GROSS win rate, return per day, worst trade, drawdown at its live slice, every year and set, and the deflated Sharpe ratio for the trials behind it — per trade, and on its MONTHS (its episodes; PREREG183's `bank.dsr_months`: López de Prado, Lipton & Zoonekynd's best-of-K test with the months' autocorrelation, skew and kurtosis) — and, on the combination, how long the live record needs (`bank.track_record`). THE BAR (user directives 2026-10-15: "test long arms against the market's average daily rise. The idea is to beat the market but I'm also ok with using 3x for trades we are confident the market will go up (or down).", and 2026-10-16: "if we pick a 3x ETF position in the right direction, this should be considered a good pick. So, it's not only about beating the market, it's also about following it with 3x leverage ...
