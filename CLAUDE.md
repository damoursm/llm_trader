## THE LIVE ACCOUNT — one $10,000 account, the best combination only (from 2026-10-09)

User directive 2026-10-09: "... from now on, only the best combination model will be used on the 10000$ account. If we want to test other strategies in live, we'll use another 10000$ account." The combination is PREREG57's best (`memory/model-combination-search-2026-10.md`, report https://claude.ai/artifact/3eY5F8UWDZTg2tpEqcpGz6). THE RULE: the $10,000 simulated account trades ONLY these books — the selection short's vol, ETF and fall arms (`sel_short_traded_arms`), the dip long book and, from the 2026-10-12 session, the crash-rebound book (below); every other strategy runs as SHADOW (the model arm and vol2 are still scored and journaled, their picks settled `shadow_arm`, never traded; the ETF dual-momentum book and the thin arm stay off); a strategy to test live gets ANOTHER $10,000 account. THE COMBINATION GROWS BY ARMS — each for its own side, horizon and market, active when its own conditions occur (Standing rules: THE COMBINATION'S DESIGN, user directive 2026-10-11).

THE ACCOUNT (`src/performance/live_account.py`, `enable_live_account`, from 2026-10-09; tests `tests/test_live_account.py`): ONE simulated IBKR margin account for every book — equity = `sel_short_account_initial` $10,000 + the dollar P&L of every trade it funded (the shorts' `sel_account_shares`, the dip longs' `dip_account_shares`, the crash long's `crash_account_shares`; shares × entry × the ledger's net return), ONE margin room = equity − the initial requirement of everything it holds (a short: the larger of Reg T's tiers and IBKR's house rate, below; a long: Reg T's 50%). Each short 1/its ARM's slice of the equity — vol 1/20, ETF 1/16, fall 1/12 from the 2026-10-12 session (PREREG80, the user's choice 2026-10-10: "More growth, deeper drawdown"; `sim_account.arm_slices`, `sel_short_{vol,etf,fall}_account_slices`; `sim_account.size` fed the shared state) — each dip long 1/10 (`live_account.size_long`: within the room, the longs' value at most the equity, at most 10 open); the crash book's TQQQ long (on QQQ's decisions) 1/4 at its fund's 75% margin rate (`live_account.size_crash`, one at a time, selling the dip book's latest longs whole when that makes room, `crash_give_way_plan`). THE DIP BOOK GIVES WAY: a short the room cannot fund in full first sells the dip book's longs WHOLE, the latest first, only as many as needed — none when selling them all would not let the short open one more share (the shorts' own initial requirement past the equity; PREREG64) (`give_way_plan`; exit reason `dip_gave_way`; never bought back; whole positions keep one ledger trade per broker position — the engine's "just enough shares" variant measured the same). DE-LEVERAGING: after the books' exits, at a tick where the maintenance requirement exceeds `live_account_delever_trigger` 80% of equity, the dip longs are sold, the latest first, whole, until it is at most `live_account_delever_target` 60% (`dip_delever`; never bought back). A MARGIN CALL (equity under the maintenance requirement) closes every short and long the account funded (`sel_margin_call` / `dip_margin_call` / `crash_margin_call`). One check a pass, `tracker.monitor_live_account`, after the books' exits — at the start of the tick (live path) and in the end-of-tick block. Chosen by PREREG57 (on the old simulator; the faller shorts' addition held out of sample on the weekly walk-forward, +6.3 log pp a year). On the simulator that mimics production (`Mimic()` + V6 — the account as deployed from 2026-10-14: V6's extras, the fall and ETF turns, the vol arm's 4× cover without its volatility exit and its 125% give-back, the ETF arm's whole give-back without the VIX-linked products, each short arm its own slice; 20 stratified draws, PREREG94's borrow construction — PREREG102's `LIVE_S3` draws, `python -m src.backtest.bank`), on THE POOLED HISTORY 2018-01-02 – 2026-09-24 (Standing rules: THE EVALUATION STANDARD): +48.5% a year (draws +39.8 … +58.1), worst drawdown 28% (37% in the worst draw), 78% of months positive (the median month +2.5%, the worst −16%, −27% in the worst draw), the worst 12 months +7.8% on average over the draws, every calendar year positive on average — 2018 +20% (SPY −6%, price only), 2019 +16% (+29%), 2020 +73% (+16%), 2021 +48% (+27%), 2022 +39% (−20%), 2023 +19% (+24%), 2024 +36% (+23%; −0.7% in the worst draw), 2025 +120% (+16%), 2026 to September +96% (+13%) —, a margin call in 10 draws of 20, all in sets A (1) and B (9). ## THE SELECTION SHORT (from 2026-09-28)

The live account's shorts: the vol, ETF and fall arms (`sel_short_traded_arms`); the model and vol2 arms run as SHADOW. - **The pick rule** (every arm). Each arm ranks its universe on EVERY regular-hours 30-minute bar — the base universe is the 3,430 deep-store names with a 20-session mean regular-hours dollar volume ≥ $5M (~2,150 a day, fixed at `--prepare`) whose bar price is ≥ $5 (`sel_short.decide`: the price floor applies to each bar, not at the prepare). The bar's TOP-1 is taken when FRESH (a new high against its own scores over the arm's window of previous sessions — `sel_short_own_window_days` 30 for the model, 20 for the others — or fewer than `sel_short_own_min_history` (10) of them) and it is the name's first fresh pick of the day — `eval_metrics.selection_entries`' rule, pinned by a parity test.
- **The model arm** (SHADOW since 2026-10-09: scored and journaled, its picks settled `shadow_arm`, never traded). The LightGBM tail-regression model **V2** (`cache/ml/sel/final/fx_v2_le2026-04-30.txt`, fit on sessions ≤ 2026-04-30 over today's names and the companies delisted since 2021; 161 inputs — 85 base OHLCV / pivot-leg features of the 30-minute bar, 61 per-ticker deep features and 15 from `src/signals/sel_v2.py`, per bar, per session (computed at the prepare, `extras/<day>.json`) and across the bar's names; the 8 yfinance earnings / analyst inputs BLANKED, as in training) ranks the base universe, scored in ONE place, `sel_short.score_v2`. v1 (`tailreg_long_400`) is kept as `model_v1.*` with its history in `scores_v1/` — the rollback. Known gaps of its pre-open snapshot, not fixed: FINRA's short-volume ratio of D−2 where training read D−1 (`dp_sv_ratio_*`), pre-market bars that land after the build missing from `dp_xh_pre_*`. Evidence: `memory/v2-model-arm-deploy-2026-10.md`, `memory/overnight-models-2026-10.md`.
- **The vol arm** (user directive 2026-09-26: "Deploy 'Short the most volatile name' … alongside the current short model … over time we'll evaluate which one is the best"; `enable_sel_short_vol`). The same run applies the SAME rule to each name's 30-minute ATR% (`sel_short_vol_feature` = `atr_pct_14`, a base feature the scorer already computes; no deep snapshot needed): top-1, fresh against its own ATR% history over `sel_short_vol_own_window_days` (20) sessions (`scores_vol/`; user directive 2026-09-27 — the model keeps 30), its first fresh pick of the day, then the trade rule below. ## Running the pipeline

```bash
python main.py                # Run once, console output only
python main.py --email        # Run once and send email report
python main.py --schedule     # Poll-loop runner
python main.py --supervise    # PRODUCTION (scripts/run_scheduler.bat): --schedule under an auto-restart supervisor
python main.py --dashboard    # Read-only monitoring dashboard (Plotly Dash via waitress)
python main.py --backfill     # Pre-warm OHLCV caches for the whole universe, then exit
```

**Slots** (NYSE market days; the poll loop reads the clock every `scheduler_poll_seconds` 5 s): RTH every 30 min 09:30–16:00 ET; extended per `extended_windows` (`04:00-07:00@60,07:00-09:30,16:00-17:30,18:00-19:00@60,19:50-19:50`); overnight on the overnight venue's Sun–Thu nights (20:30, 21:30, 22:30, 23:30, 01:00, 02:00, 03:00, 03:30). Every slot is a full trading tick (`extended_hours_mode` / `overnight_hours_mode` = trade). An email goes out every slot (`SCHEDULER_EMAIL_EVERY_TICK=true`), plus `email_on_problem` on any broker/LLM/price/scorer health problem.

**Background slots.** EOD maintenance at/after 16:20 ET on market days runs as its OWN PROCESS (`python -m src.scheduler.eod --day D`, launched by `runner._eod_poll`; in a scheduler thread its replay starved the ticks): the IBKR Flex borrow fees (own thread) beside cache warm → `simulated_trades` retention → replay materialisation → walk-forward calibration → shape-history append (pinned to the market day) → tier-2 backtest tail → IBKR BID_ASK spread-sweep subprocess → the live record against the simulator (`live_calibration`, below); `eod_replay_refresh_days` 45. Below normal priority, an OS lock (`cache/eod/eod.lock`), its own log (`logs/eod_<day>.log`, console `logs/eod_console.log`); every step's outcome in `cache/eod/state/<day>.json` — a relaunched scheduler or a reboot resumes the due day's chain (today's after 16:20, else the previous market day's) at its first step without an outcome (an interrupted step reruns, a failed one does not; at most 3 launches a day per scheduler process); the scheduler logs each finished step and, once the chain finishes, refreshes its win-rate and market-relative calibrations. THE DATABASE FENCE (`src/scheduler/db_fence.py`: DuckDB admits one read-write process and the scheduler opens everything read-write): the scheduler raises `cache/eod/tick_fence.json` `eod_tick_fence_lead_seconds` (120) before each slot and for the whole tick; the EOD process opens no connection while it stands, and a tick waits for an EOD query already running (`db_busy.json`, at most `eod_db_wait_seconds` 300); a flag whose process is dead is ignored; the EOD process exits after `eod_timeout_seconds` (20 h). `python -m src.scheduler.eod --day D --status` / `--steps replay,backtest` (rerun). The deep history refresh at 23:45 ET EVERY night incl. weekends (subprocess `python -m src.data.deep.refresh`, budget 14,400 s). The deep pre-open run at 08:30 ET on market days (`--profile preopen`, budget 2,700 s: the fast families in concurrent lanes, the 30-minute store through the previous session, the session snapshot). Both deep slots run on their OWN timer thread (`runner._start_deep_slot_timer`, 15 s), never behind a tick. The auto-refactor (02:00) and the weekly ML retrain (Saturday 08:00) are HELD.

**Supervisor and watchdogs.** `--supervise` relaunches the scheduler when it exits; a HANG becomes an exit through the broker-sync watchdog (`broker_sync_watchdog_seconds` 600) or the whole-tick watchdog (`tick_watchdog_seconds` 2700, moved back by the time a due slot's live path takes inside the tick); every tick-path yfinance call carries `timeout=30`. **To STOP a supervised scheduler, kill the `--supervise` parent FIRST, then the `--schedule` child. To restart, use `scripts/restart_all.ps1`** — never an ad-hoc `python.exe` (that is how duplicate schedulers appear). The scheduler's subprocesses (the sel_short scorer, the pre-open run, the deep refresh, the EOD process and its spread sweep) survive a scheduler kill by design: file logs and OS/heartbeat locks, never pipes. `Stop-ScheduledTask` (what `restart_all.ps1` does) takes them down with the task's process tree: to deploy scheduler code while one runs, kill only the `--schedule` child just before a slot — the supervisor relaunches it in 5 s with the new code.

**OHLCV caches.** `--backfill` is additive into `cache/ohlcv/` (daily, full depth from Polygon; weekly is resampled on demand). `--backfill-30m` warms the 30-minute TICK cache `cache/ohlcv_30m/`, capped at save time to `intraday_30m_max_bars` 3,380 (260 sessions × 13). In RTH a cached frame is fresh once it holds the latest COMPLETED bar (`market_data._latest_completed_30m_start`). The email's six research evaluations are reused within a day for `email_eval_cache_seconds` (6 h; the pipeline's call only). The deep 2021→ 30-minute history is a separate store, `cache/ml/bars30m_deep/` (`python -m src.data.intraday_store --extend`; extended daily by the pre-open run and the sel_short prepare).

Logs → `logs/llm_trader_YYYY-MM-DD.log` (7-day rotation). To force a fresh run, delete `cache/news_*.json` / `cache/snapshots_*.json`. **Never delete casually:** `cache/ohlcv/`, `data/llm_trader.db`, `data/live_features/`, `data/ibkr_borrow/`, `data/ibkr_flex/`, `cache/ml/sel_short/scores/` + `scores_vol/` + `scores_etf/` + `scores_thin/` + `scores_vol2/` + `scores_fall/` + `scores_v1/`.

**Bars and prices.** The trailing NaN-Close bar yfinance appends is trimmed at parse time (`cache.trim_trailing_nan_bars` inside `load_ohlcv`) — scoring must never bypass `get_history` (`tests/test_scorer_contract.py`). ## Architecture

### Pipeline flow (`src/pipeline.py`, `run_pipeline()`)

```
0.  Universe        — (SHADOW) static watchlist + discovery; the discovery gate drops junk, exotic types and names
                      below the OBSERVATION floor ($1 / $1M).
1.  News            — (SHADOW) every feed → the merged, archived article pool → per-ticker digests scored by the
                      local Qwen.
2.  Snapshots       — Polygon batch (live + NBBO) → yfinance → grouped-daily close.
3.  Context, scores — (SHADOW) smart money, macro, regime & timing, the method scorers in [-1, +1].
4.  Aggregation     — (SHADOW) the ML-stacker combine → TickerSignal.
5.  Recommendations — (SHADOW) the rank rule: computed, gated, persisted, never opened.
5S. THE LIVE BOOKS  — the selection short, the dip long and the crash-rebound books. The scorer subprocess and the
                      borrow-file fetch start at tick start and the WHOLE live path runs FIRST, before Step 0: the
                      marks, every live book's exits, the live account's check, the dip fills and the crash buy, the
                      dip orders, the shorts' entries (after the scorer's live arms) and a broker sync; Step 0
                      pins the trades open BEFORE those entries (the tick's new shorts never join its shadow
                      universe). A slot due while the shadow steps below still run gets its own live path on time
                      (`run_live_path`, its broker problems logged CRITICAL).
6.  Tracking        — the ledger marks, the live books' steps again, the (empty) legacy exit stack, the broker sync.
7.  Charts + email.
```

**Liquidity.** Each live book applies its own floors (the shorts: price ≥ $5 at the bar and a 20-session mean RTH dollar volume ≥ $5M from the deep store; the dip book: ≥ $1B a day; the crash book trades QQQ). The shadow pipeline's: the OBSERVATION floor ($1 / $1M) admits names to scoring, the TRADE floor (Gate 4: `trade_min_price` $5 / `trade_min_dollar_volume` $5M, `pipeline._is_tradeable`) screens its recommendations. ONE dollar-volume definition elsewhere: `liquidity.dollar_volume` = the mean Close×Volume of the last 20 valid bars (`spread.adv_of` keeps its median-60 basis — the cost classes were fitted on it). Tests: `tests/test_trade_liquidity_gate.py`, `tests/test_dead_tickers.py`.

**IBKR BID_ASK spread sweep** (`src/performance/spread_sweep.py`): an EOD subprocess (clientId `ibkr_client_id`+50) → `cache/ibkr_spread.json`, rotating stalest first under a 900 s budget; works without paid market-data lines; it feeds the shadow liquidity forecast (`src/performance/liquidity_forecast.py` → `signals.exp_halfspread_bps`). `python -m src.performance.spread_sweep [--probe]`.

### Performance and the ledger

- **Per-trade return** (`tracker._pct_return`): cost-aware buy-and-hold (`cost_in` / `cost_out` = half-spread + commission; SELL mirrors BUY); open trades are marked live.
- **Costs per leg** (`spread.resolve_leg_cost`, `sim_per_trade_cost_attribution`): the leg's own fill → the same-tick average of fills in its liquidity class → the tick average → the time-of-day period average in its class (shrunk) → the period average → `_one_side_cost` (the calibrated real-fill overrides; the hand-built spread formula only on a fills-free database or for sub-minimum-price legs). Commission is a fee CEILING (`ibkr_fixed` max($1, $0.005/share), ×1.5). `calibrate_sim_costs` runs before each tick's mark and entry steps. Tests: `tests/test_cost_buckets.py`.
- **Short borrow carry** (`enable_short_borrow_cost`): every stock short pays IBKR's fee DAY BY DAY (user directive 2026-10-06: "we want the real exact borrow fees we would have in live trading a real account and we want that data to be as complete as possible"; `src/performance/borrow_fees.py`, `enable_ibkr_borrow_schedule`): every calendar day from the short's settlement to the cover's — IBKR's trade date (an overnight-session execution belongs to the next session), T+1 on days that settle (Columbus and Veterans Day trade but never settle) — shares × roundup(1.02 × the prior session's close, to the dollar) × THAT day's rate / 360. Each day, in order: what IBKR charged our account (`broker_borrow_fees`, per share); IBKR's own rate with the formula — our archive's last file of the day, IBKR's API bar, the community archive (`borrow_history.fee_daily`), carried over a day none holds for at most a week (or from around the entry), the file in force for a day not over (provisional); the entry stamp; `short_borrow_annual_pct` 3%. Stored on the trade (`borrow_days` [day, source, rate, collateral price, fee per share], `borrow_fee_ps` / `borrow_fee_usd`, `borrow_rate_now`, `borrow_final`) and charged by `_pct_return`, the daily NAV (each day in its interval) and so the simulated account. A short CLOSED before the schedule existed keeps that flat carry until `borrow_backfill_closed` — the user's condition: the formula backfills only once checked against IBKR's own charges (`python -m src.performance.borrow_fees --verify`). No look-ahead: a day not over reads only the file in force and closes final from 16:15 ET (`tests/test_no_lookahead.py`). Tests: `tests/test_borrow_fees.py`.
- **IBKR borrow book** (`src/data/ibkr_borrow.py`): IBKR's public short-stock file (`ftp2.interactivebrokers.com`, user `shortstock`; shares available + annual fee for ~19,750 symbols) is downloaded in every tick's Step-1 pool and archived RAW in `data/ibkr_borrow/<ET date>/<HHMMSS>.txt.gz` (never delete — IBKR keeps no history); `borrow_at(ticker, when)` reads the file in force at any instant. Every NEW equity short is checked when opened (`enable_short_borrow_gate`): the selection short needs ≥ $10k available at any fee. No snapshot within 120 min → a new short WAITS for one (`ibkr_borrow_require_file`; off: not checked, warned); futures / indices / FX / crypto are never judged. Tests: `tests/test_ibkr_borrow.py`.
- **Daily NAV** (`daily_nav.compute_compound_return`): a path-faithful daily walk over cached closes, capital-weighted per day and compounded; deterministic given the database.
- **Splits during a hold** (`tracker.apply_splits`, `enable_split_adjustment`; PREREG64, user 2026-10-09: "Fix the bugs in production"): from 04:00 ET of a split's execution date — the pre-market open; the overnight hours before it still quote the old terms — every OPEN trade is rescaled through the splits executed since its entry (the split data `intraday_store.split_rows`: Polygon lists announced splits ahead, refreshed nightly), as IBKR rescales the position: the price fields (`SPLIT_PRICE_FIELDS`: entry, reference close, fill, target, the pick's closes, the dip's close / trend / limit / ATR, the crash long's close / trend) x the factor, the share counts (`SPLIT_SHARE_FIELDS`: the accounts' shares, the broker's requested and filled quantities) / it — exact, a reverse split's fraction kept (IBKR pays it in cash; the broker's counts truncate); the dollars, the return and the cost fractions unchanged. Applied at every ledger load (`_load_trades`) and the broker's own (`reconcile.sync`), once (`split_factor`, the values before it in `split_orig`, `split_log`); the current mark carried from the terms of its own time; a fill IBKR reports later for an order submitted before the split (`reconcile._split_k`) and IBKR's borrow charges of earlier days (per share of THEIR day) come into the same terms; the squeeze cover reads the original entry times the factor (never twice). Without it a 1-for-10 reverse split read as a 900% loss on a short — a false margin call of the whole account — and the broker's share counts no longer matched IBKR's. Tests `tests/test_splits.py`.
- **Win rates are GROSS** (standing directive): a win is the right direction on raw prices (`tracker.gross_return_pct`, `is_gross_win`, `gross_win_rate`); costs live in the RETURN metrics only. Pinned by `tests/test_gross_winrate_convention.py`.

### Deep history store (`src/data/deep/`) and deep features

**The store** holds point-in-time external history: SEC filings + XBRL facts + Form 3/4/5 + 13F roll-ups + fails-to-deliver; Polygon extended-hours bars / short interest / short volume / dividends / splits / news + provider sentiment / delisted names / IPOs / ticker details; ALFRED macro vintages; yfinance indices / earnings / analyst actions / shares; Fama-French; DIX; COT; Wikipedia pageviews; Quiver congress / lobbying / contracts / dark pool; the Reg SHO threshold lists and IBKR's borrow per name per day (below). ### Key constraints

- **Polygon** (`src/data/polygon_client.py`) is the PRIMARY equity price source: snapshots with the live NBBO, bars, news, reference data. `_get` retries once on a transport error or 5xx (never on 403 / 404 / 429); a 403 is logged once per endpoint family. `to_polygon_symbol()` maps `BRK-B` → `BRK.B` at every per-ticker call site (the hyphen form silently returns nothing).
- **yfinance is structurally required** for what Polygon does not serve: indices (^VIX / ^MOVE / ^TICK — the shadow Macro Regime Filter), futures, options chains, fundamentals / analyst / earnings. On a 429 it backs off 60 → 120 → 240 s and stops after 3 failures. Its `download` is SERIALIZED process-wide (`src/data/yf_guard.py`, installed when `market_data` loads): yfinance 1.2.0 keeps every call's results in ONE module-wide dict that each call resets, so two downloads at once (the tick's Steps 1-3 pool runs several) returned each other's tickers or waited forever — the 2026-10-12 01:00 tick hung in `bond_internals` until the watchdog. `ENABLE_IBKR_PRICE_FEED=false`: this account has no API entitlement for live US quotes.
- **Two price paths.** The bulk snapshot feeds analysis, scoring and the price-provenance check (its NBBO comes from `lastQuote`). Execution prices come from `tracker._fetch_price` (RTH: Polygon last trade → yfinance; off-RTH: yfinance 1-min prepost → Polygon). ### Broker integration

`src/broker/` is the real execution leg beside the internal ledger ("shadow & reconcile"). Modes `off | dry_run | ibkr_paper | ibkr_live` (`broker_mode`, live: `ibkr_paper`, port 4002; the factory refuses paper on a known live port). Interactive Brokers through `ib_async` (imported lazily) → IB Gateway under IBC (`docs/DEPLOYMENT.md`).

- **`reconcile.sync()`** runs TWICE per tick — in the live path right after the selection short's entries (the report persisted at once), and at the end of the tick (a late scorer's picks, a same-tick resend of anything the first left unfilled; the two reports are merged) — idempotent: **(0)** fill refresh by `orderRef`; **(0b)** tick-scoped order lifetime — unfilled orders are cancelled and their legs cleared; the live books' entries (`books.is_live`) are re-anchored and resent while their row is open (the price-aware gate, `broker_resubmit_max_adverse_bps`, is for legacy entries, and an unfilled legacy entry is never sent: `LEGACY_ENTRY_SHADOWED`); exits are never gated; **(0c)** cancel-on-close; **(1)** entries size the GAP against any same-side holding NO OTHER open trade owns (`_owned_by_open`: the selection short stacks several open trades on one name, 2026-09-28); **(2)** closed trades still holding a position are closed from the actual position MINUS what open trades in the ticker own and MINUS the shares already being covered (the unfilled rest of working covers, covers sent earlier in the pass) — two closed trades on one name never cover it twice; a dead entry leg stays parked only when shares no other open trade owns back it; a closed trade whose own entry never filled sends nothing (`NEVER_FILLED`, terminal) and no position row means flat (no order) — never an exit sized from the recorded fill; an order IBKR REFUSES outright is resent in the same tick (`broker_refused_resends_per_tick`) and parked for the next (an entry is given up after `broker_refused_max_ticks`, an exit never); **(3)** drift → `broker_drift_action="flatten"` (never adopted into the ledger; `ibkr_live` always refuses auto-flatten); **(4)** settle — fill fast or kill (`broker_settle_seconds` 30, re-anchored every ~6 s).
- **Invariant**: no order works the book unless a ledger leg points at it — `_persist_legs` flushes after every submission, `cancel_order` cancels every order with a ref, a pre-submit duplicate guard, an orphan sweep, `reqAllOpenOrders()` on connect. Tests: `tests/test_broker_reliability.py`, `tests/test_broker_price_aware_resubmit.py`.
- **Reliability**: transient failures retry (`broker_submit_retries` 2 × 5 s); auto-reconnect (60 s cooldown); `IB.RequestTimeout` 45 s; the 600 s sync watchdog. Gateway auto-recovery (`broker_gateway_auto_restart`, paper only) is armed by the wedge detector: 5 consecutive request timeouts force a recycle, and 3 recycles within `broker_wedge_recycle_window_seconds` (3600) restart the gateway; `get_account()` (served from the local cache) never counts as an answer; the recycle history persists in `cache/broker_wedge_recycles.json`. A gateway with no API listener that is younger than `broker_gateway_boot_grace_seconds` (120; a relaunch opens its port ~25–45 s after start) is BOOTING — the recovery waits for its port instead of killing it, without consuming the cooldown (2026-09-29 03:02: a 22-s-old gateway was killed and relaunched); older and still no port = stuck → restarted. The relaunch (`gateway_recovery._relaunch`) waits for the killed gateway's `IBC Gateway` task instance to END before `schtasks /Run` — the task's IgnoreNew policy silently drops a run while StartGateway.bat is still closing (4 of 5 recoveries 2026-09-29..10-05 came back only at its 10-minute keep-alive) — ends one stuck past 20 s, and keeps a gateway the keep-alive already started. Tests: `tests/test_broker_reconnect.py`, `tests/test_gateway_recovery.py`.
- **Orders**: a short entry under Rule 201 is offered one tick above the bid and rests until the next tick (above, `broker_ssr`); otherwise marketable `LMT` at the model price ± the session cap — `broker_limit_cap_bps` 45 in RTH (spread-aware up to 400 with a live book), 80 extended, 150 overnight; off-RTH forces LMT + `outsideRth`; class shares normalise `BRK-B` ↔ `BRK B`.
- **IBKR's statement** (`src/broker/flex.py`, user directive 2026-10-06): the EOD chain fetches, in its own thread (IBKR can take hours to generate a statement; one still generating is collected first on the next run, `data/ibkr_flex/pending.json`), the Activity Flex Query `ibkr_flex_query_id` with the Flex Web Service token `ibkr_flex_token` (both from `.env`; skipped while empty; the statement is asked ONLY from the host IBKR's SendRequest names — gdcdyn, whose name fails to resolve here for minutes at a time, is then reached through the shared CDN edge with its name in TLS and the Host header, never swapped for another host; up to an hour of polling, its reference kept in `pending.json` from the moment IBKR issues it) — its Borrow Fees Details section is every fee IBKR charged the account, per short position per value date (quantity, price, value, rate, fee) → `broker_borrow_fees` (each statement period replaced whole, so a re-fetch never doubles a day) + the raw XML in `data/ibkr_flex/` (never delete). The ledger's borrow schedule takes these charges over its formula. `python -m src.broker.flex --borrow-fees`. Tests: `tests/test_borrow_fees.py`.

- **THE EVALUATION STANDARD — ONE POOLED HISTORY, A BANK OF MODELS** (user directive 2026-10-14: "Instead of checking for the three different sets (A0, A, and B), check for the full dataset (A0+A+B). Each individual model needs to give growth on that whole set but doesn't need to have growth all the time. I.e., a model could be good only under certain market conditions that you will need to find. So, some of the models might need more filters or ways to adapt to the market. What's important is that over the years 2018-2026 it has performed well when it was activated and didn't produce a drawback that was too high. This means that winrate and return per day are important metrics. In an effort to not overtfit, we also need to have a enough trades made so that the results are significant, reliable, and transferable to out of sample data. To make sure the combination algorithm still has good yearly and monthly growth, we then need multiple models in our bank of models so that we cover all market conditions."). THE POOLED HISTORY = sets A0 + A + B (`periods.POOLED`), decisions 2018-01-02 – 2026-09-24: every decision about a model or the combination reads it WHOLE; the sets stay the simulator's construction (each its own borrow data, Evaluation: THE TEST SETS) and are reported beside the calendar years and the market contexts, never decided on alone; until one continuous run spans them (PREREG105's rebuilt stores) the pooled numbers chain the sets' accounts — a pooled rate is the years-weighted mean of their log rates (`periods.pool_weights`), a pooled drawdown the worst of theirs. `python -m src.backtest.bank` → `cache/ml/bank/<day>.json` applies it mechanically (`src/backtest/bank.py`, `tests/test_bank.py`). (1) A MODEL — an arm — belongs in THE BANK when, on the pooled history, its own trades (the reliability cards: each arm alone at the live costs) show ENOUGH TRADES — at least 100 in at least 30 distinct entry months (a month's trades share its market: the months are its independent episodes) —, SIGNIFICANCE — the 95% interval of its RETURN PER DAY (a bootstrap over entry months) above 0 —, TRANSFERABILITY — its average trade positive in both chronological halves of its trades and with ANY one calendar year left out — and GROWTH — the arm alone at its live slice of a $10,000 account grows over the whole history; beside them its GROSS win rate, return per day, worst trade, drawdown at its live slice, every year and set, and the deflated Sharpe ratio for the trials behind it. Short of trades only it is INSUFFICIENT (not proven, not refuted); short of any other check it FAILS. A daily-bar arm whose data reach before 2018 (the dip book from 2007, the crash book's QQQ from 1988: PREREG103's long history) takes the earlier years when 2018–2026 hold too few trades. (2) IT NEED NOT EARN ALL THE TIME: its card maps WHERE it earns — its trades by SPY's trend (above / below the 200-session average) and SPY's 20-session realized-volatility tercile at the entry, and their six cells; a cell of at least 30 trades whose interval lies above 0 EARNS, below 0 LOSES. A context it loses in is the next study: a FILTER or an ADAPTATION (its size, its exit) written into the arm's own rule from point-in-time inputs (the whole-history terciles are a label for reading, never a decision input; a no-look-ahead guard), few parameters at a plateau's middle, pre-registered, and justified by its TRADES — the trades a filter removes number at least 30 in at least 10 months and their return per day's interval lies below 0 (`bank.filter_test`) — or by RISK (fewer margin calls, a shallower drawdown: the user's call); a filter, threshold or parameter CHOSEN on the history is measured out of time — each year from 2020 decided on the years before it only (`bank.walk_forward`), its decay reported. (3) THE COMBINATION must grow every year and every month as far as the market allows, so the bank must cover every market context: it is judged on the pooled growth a year, every calendar year against SPY and QQQ, the MONTHS (the share of positive months, the worst month, the worst twelve months, the longest time under water), the worst drawdown and the margin calls, and its COVERAGE (`bank.coverage`: its months by the context at their first session — the average month, the share positive, the growth a year, each arm's share of the P&L); a context where it is idle or loses is where a new arm is worth the most (THE COMBINATION'S DESIGN). (4) A CHANGE is decided by the pooled add rule (Evaluation: DEPLOYING). (5) Out of sample after the fact: set D (the forward holdout) and the live record (set C) are never tuned on; when a verdict is unsure, go deeper rather than decide (user directive 2026-10-14: "If unsure about new rules performing well or not, go deeper on the historical data") — more draws, the longest history the arm's data allow, set D as it accrues.
