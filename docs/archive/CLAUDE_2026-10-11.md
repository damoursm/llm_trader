THE RULE: the $10,000 simulated account trades ONLY these books — the selection short's vol, ETF and fall arms (`sel_short_traded_arms`), the dip long book and, from the 2026-10-12 session, the crash-rebound book (below); every other strategy runs as SHADOW (the model arm and vol2 are still scored and journaled, their picks settled `shadow_arm`, never traded; the ETF dual-momentum book and the thin arm stay off); a strategy to test live gets ANOTHER $10,000 account. THE COMBINATION GROWS BY ARMS — each for its own side, horizon and market, active when its own conditions occur (Standing rules: THE COMBINATION'S DESIGN, user directive 2026-10-11).

THE ACCOUNT (`src/performance/live_account.py`, `enable_live_account`, from 2026-10-09; tests `tests/test_live_account.py`): ONE simulated IBKR margin account for every book — equity = `sel_short_account_initial` $10,000 + the dollar P&L of every trade it funded (the shorts' `sel_account_shares`, the dip longs' `dip_account_shares`, the crash long's `crash_account_shares`; shares × entry × the ledger's net return), ONE margin room = equity − the initial requirement of everything it holds (a short: the larger of Reg T's tiers and IBKR's house rate, below; a long: Reg T's 50%). Each short 1/its ARM's slice of the equity — vol 1/20, ETF 1/16, fall 1/12 from the 2026-10-12 session (PREREG80, the user's choice 2026-10-10: "More growth, deeper drawdown"; `sim_account.arm_slices`, `sel_short_{vol,etf,fall}_account_slices`; `sim_account.size` fed the shared state) — each dip long 1/10 (`live_account.size_long`: within the room, the longs' value at most the equity, at most 10 open); the crash book's QQQ long 1/4 (`live_account.size_crash`, one at a time, selling the dip book's latest longs whole when that makes room, `crash_give_way_plan`). THE DIP BOOK GIVES WAY: a short the room cannot fund in full first sells the dip book's longs WHOLE, the latest first, only as many as needed — none when selling them all would not let the short open one more share (the shorts' own initial requirement past the equity; PREREG64) (`give_way_plan`; exit reason `dip_gave_way`; never bought back — the engine's "just enough shares" variant measured the same on this combination: +78.9 vs +77.0 %/yr mean over the starts, +56.1 vs +55.4 from 2021, no margin call either way, and whole positions keep one ledger trade per broker position). DE-LEVERAGING: after both books' exits, at a tick where the maintenance requirement exceeds `live_account_delever_trigger` 80% of equity, the dip longs are sold, the latest first, whole, until it is at most `live_account_delever_target` 60% (`dip_delever`; never bought back). A MARGIN CALL (equity under the maintenance requirement) closes every short and long the account funded (`sel_margin_call` / `dip_margin_call`). One check a pass, `tracker.monitor_live_account`, after both books' exits — at the start of the tick (live path) and in the end-of-tick block. Chosen by PREREG57 (on the old simulator; the faller shorts' addition held out of sample on the weekly walk-forward, +6.3 log pp a year). On the simulator that mimics production (`Mimic()` + V6 — the account as deployed for the 2026-10-12 session: V6's extras, the fall and ETF turns, the vol arm's 4× cover without its volatility exit, the ETF arm's whole give-back, each short arm its own slice; five draws; set B's borrow from the PREREG67 models), by borrow-data test set (Standing rules): set A (with IBKR's borrow file, decisions 2021-02-01 – 2024-06-24) +35.8% a year, worst drawdown 16%, 0.391% a day; set B (without it, to 2026-09-24) +106.7%, drawdown 23%, 0.475% a day; no margin call on either (with the crash book, PREREG86's `final86.py`; without it +35.5% / +105.8%, PREREG80; at 1/24 for every short +28.3% / +90.4%, drawdowns 15% / 20%; before PREREG77's four changes +26.2% / +58.0%; `memory/breadth-vs-sizing-2026-10.md`).

### Signals and the combine (SHADOW)

Everything in this section runs every tick and is persisted to the `signals` panel; it drives only the shadow rank rule.

- **Score-sign convention** (the invariant the combine, IC and inversion depend on): every method's sign is the predicted direction of the STOCK (+ up, − down); mean-reversion methods bake the reversal into the sign; |score| is confidence.
- **Weighted methods** (`aggregator._BASE_WEIGHTS`): news 0.40; tech, insider 0.30; sector_momentum 0.20; pattern, momentum 0.18; massive, put_call, oi_skew, money_flow, trend_strength, pead, ext_gap, mom_12_1, hi52 0.15; iv_rank 0.13; sent_velocity, max_pain, vwap, iv_expr, coint, st_reversal, ml_ohlcv 0.12; news_quiet, rsi2_rev, dloc_rev 0.10; news_bull_fresh, market_momentum 0.08; news_bear_fresh, catalyst_tilt 0.06; news_shock 0.04 (`broker_advisor` keeps a 0.10 weight but is OFF). **A weighted method needs FOUR registrations**: the base weight, an `_raw_active` entry (without it `_normalised_weights` gives it weight 0 while it scores, persists and ranks — `tests/test_news_quiet.py` pins it), `agreement.METHOD_FAMILIES`, and — to reach DIRECTION — `ml_stacker.STACKER_SIGNED_FEATURES`: the stackers are the combine, so a base weight alone reaches only coherence, `sources_agreeing` and the family votes.
- **The ML stackers ARE the combine** (`src/analysis/ml_stacker.py`, `ml_combine_arm_share` 1.0; the weighted camps are the per-side fail-soft, recorded per ticker in `combine_source`). Frozen 2026-08-24 logistic artifacts (`stacker_model_class`). 54 features: 31 SIGNED (the weighted method scores incl. `ml_ohlcv`, `tape_score`, the news family and `news_raw_score`) + 23 unsigned context (news mass / count, `atr_pct` / `bb_width_pct` / `vol_ratio`, an 18-class catalyst one-hot). Confidence and its factors are EXCLUDED (computed downstream of the combine; a test pins it). News features enter as the within-run centered rank over the Gate-4 pool (`stacker_news_basis="rank"`; `centered_rank_pooled` mirrors the aggregator's transform minus shaping), but serving dispatches on the ARTIFACT's `news_basis` stamp — the frozen artifacts stamp `absolute`. The news columns the panel mask cannot reach are masked at the news epoch (`enable_stacker_news_epoch_mask`). ⚠ `ml_ohlcv` (30-minute rows since 2026-09-19) reaches the frozen stackers at ~2.1× their training scale — retrain the stackers and `ml_exit` first when retrains resume. ML combine bands `ml_diff_threshold_long` / `_short` 0.0463 / 0.0374; `ml_raw_confidence_scale` 0.12 is self-calibrated as a SATURATION share (`src/signals/ml_scale.py`).
- **Rank basis** (`method_score_basis="rank"`): the combine consumes each method's CENTERED WITHIN-RUN RANK. Zeros abstain; a method with fewer than `method_rank_min_views` (5) non-zero views on tradeable names gets weight 0 for the run (override `news_quiet=3` — abstention is its mechanism — logged as `rank-thin this run`); ranks are over the Gate-4 pool with observe-only names interpolated; each method's percentile is payoff-shaped (`src/signals/rank_shaping.py`); bands `rank_diff_threshold_long` / `_short` 0.324 / 0.346, `rank_raw_confidence_scale` 0.642 — resolved ONLY in `aggregator._direction_bands` / `_raw_confidence_scale`. Consumption-time only: every persisted surface keeps the RAW score. An absolute-basis twin (`combined_score_abs` + buy/sell) rides every row.
- **Buy/sell split**: each method's effective view splits into BUY / SELL components averaged within its own camp; `combined_score` = buy − sell. The `f_*` factors and the 4 trend-predictability methods are additive overlays (`f_short_squeeze` excluded).
- **Agreement**: `METHOD_FAMILIES` rolls the methods into 7 information families; family votes and tape confirmation fold into confidence (`src/signals/agreement.py`).
- **Weighting and filters** (weights and confidence only): base → market-mode profile → win-rate adaptive → IC-informed (only methods with a pivot-basis IC t ≥ 2) → the OpEx max-pain boost. The win-rate hard filter (< 50% gross over ≥ 10 trades) and the per-SIDE filter and weights, which reach coherence, `sources_agreeing` and the family votes as well. Auto-inversion is on but `INVERTED_METHODS` is empty and the detector selects nothing. The market-relative hard filter drops only a significantly negative market-neutral ICIR. Per-method horizon states: PROVEN / DISPROVEN (dropped) / UNPROVEN (×0.5).
- **Multi-timeframe**: the 8 OHLCV methods on 30-minute, daily and weekly bars, blended 0.20 / 0.60 / 0.20 (`tf_blend_*`); the daily score stays in `*_score`, the others ride `timeframe_scores`.

Each call carries a target header (name, industry, FUND flag), salted into the cache key. **Logprob expectation** (`src/analysis/logprob_score.py`, `enable_logprob_expected_score`, top-5): the expectation over the score's digit tokens REPLACES the argmax (refused past `MAX_SHIFT` 0.25; the argmax survives as `signals.news_argmax_score`). **Catalyst-class cap** (`apply_catalyst_cap`, `catalyst_cap_classes` analyst, `catalyst_cap_limit` 0.03; the sign is never touched) is applied ONCE in `analyse_sentiment`, after the direction, the logprob replacement and `fund_catalyst_override`; the cache stores the UNCAPPED verdict (the engine loop parses with `_parse_response_uncapped`). **Passing-mention abstention** (`passing_mention_abstain_share` 0.75): abstain when ≥ 75% of a digest names the target only in article bodies; `_passing_mention_share` returns None for names whose registrant name does not resolve (ARM, ADT…). **Parser hardening**: `"score": +0.15` is repaired (anchored on `"score"`); NaN / Infinity are refused, never clamped to ±1; `news_fetcher._parse_iso` always returns aware UTC. A parse failure and an abstention are the same observable — read raw responses before judging a "no view" rate. Tests: `tests/test_direction_field.py`, `tests/test_logprob_score.py`, `tests/test_catalyst_cap.py`, `tests/test_sentiment_parse.py`.

**Relevance and scaling.** Digests are cut from the pool by COMPANY-NAME relevance (`company_names.mention_evidence` tiers `symbol_explicit` > `name_phrase` > `symbol_word` > `name_token`, the last only to confirm a feed's own tag; search feeds' tags are confirmed at fetch by `news_fetcher._confirmed_tags`; `news_relevance_min_articles` 1; no evidence → `[]`, never the whole pool). Names come from the SEC list with a Polygon fallback; security types from the bulk Polygon sweep (`prime_security_types`; Polygon's lowercase class symbols — preferreds, rights: `TpC`, `BCpC` — are skipped, upper-cased they overwrote the common stock's type); a REIT is not a fund (`industry_is_operating_trust`). `news` = the verdict × evidence MASS × source diversity, persisted at 6 decimals because the rank transform orders on it (an AST test pins it); residual ties are genuine evidence ties. Tests: `tests/test_company_names.py`, `tests/test_news_family_continuity.py`.

**The news family** (`method_epochs.NEWS_FAMILY`: news, sent_velocity, news_shock, news_bear_fresh, news_bull_fresh, catalyst_tilt, news_quiet, news_unpriced, news_unpriced_all) shares ONE scorer epoch, set from the ACTUAL restart that deploys a categorical change; the verdict cache is salted with the prompt version. `news_quiet` (0.10) takes the RAW verdict once the digest's freshest cluster is ≥ 72 h old and abstains otherwise, with `mode="time"` clustering pinned at its call site. `news_bull_fresh` (0.08) is shipped INVERTED (`news × (1 + z3/2)`, `news_bull_fresh_invert`). `news_bear_fresh` (0.06) guards a bearish read with an un-fallen tape. `catalyst_tilt` (0.06) multiplies the read by a self-calibrating per-(catalyst, side) orientation fitted on the news-event dataset (6 h refresh, asof-registered). `news_shock` (0.04) is abnormal attention against the ticker's own baseline, read only from rows at/after `news_coverage.ALL_SOURCE_SINCE`. **Story clustering** (`src/analysis/news_clustering.py`, `news_cluster_mode="hybrid"` @0.25): the time partition with same-story pieces merged, never split; IDF comes from the tick's POOL (never the digest), the target's own name is stripped, and without a corpus it degrades to the time partition. Tests: `tests/test_news_quiet.py`, `tests/test_news_bear_fresh.py`, `tests/test_catalyst_tilt.py`, `tests/test_news_clustering.py`.

### Performance and the ledger

- **Per-trade return** (`tracker._pct_return`): cost-aware buy-and-hold (`cost_in` / `cost_out` = half-spread + commission; SELL mirrors BUY); open trades are marked live.
- **Costs per leg** (`spread.resolve_leg_cost`, `sim_per_trade_cost_attribution`): the leg's own fill → the same-tick average of fills in its liquidity class → the tick average → the time-of-day period average in its class (shrunk) → the period average → `_one_side_cost` (the calibrated real-fill overrides; the hand-built spread formula only on a fills-free database or for sub-minimum-price legs). Commission is a fee CEILING (`ibkr_fixed` max($1, $0.005/share), ×1.5). `calibrate_sim_costs` runs before each tick's mark and entry steps. Tests: `tests/test_cost_buckets.py`.
- **Short borrow carry** (`enable_short_borrow_cost`): every stock short pays IBKR's fee DAY BY DAY (user directive 2026-10-06: "we want the real exact borrow fees we would have in live trading a real account and we want that data to be as complete as possible"; `src/performance/borrow_fees.py`, `enable_ibkr_borrow_schedule`): every calendar day from the short's settlement to the cover's — IBKR's trade date (an overnight-session execution belongs to the next session), T+1 on days that settle (Columbus and Veterans Day trade but never settle) — shares × roundup(1.02 × the prior session's close, to the dollar) × THAT day's rate / 360. Each day, in order: what IBKR charged our account (`broker_borrow_fees`, per share); IBKR's own rate with the formula — our archive's last file of the day, IBKR's API bar, the community archive (`borrow_history.fee_daily`), carried over a day none holds for at most a week (or from around the entry), the file in force for a day not over (provisional); the entry stamp; `short_borrow_annual_pct` 3%. Stored on the trade (`borrow_days` [day, source, rate, collateral price, fee per share], `borrow_fee_ps` / `borrow_fee_usd`, `borrow_rate_now`, `borrow_final`) and charged by `_pct_return`, the daily NAV (each day in its interval) and so the simulated account; the hypothetical rows (flips, decision streams) keep the flat rate-at-entry carry. A short CLOSED before the schedule existed keeps that flat carry until `borrow_backfill_closed` — the user's condition: the formula backfills only once checked against IBKR's own charges (`python -m src.performance.borrow_fees --verify`). Dry run on the 501 past stock shorts: 91% of their 986 charged days from IBKR's data (the rest names still queued in the fee pull); JAGX at 960 %/yr owed 9.1% of the position over 4 days where the flat carry charged 0.015%. No look-ahead: a day not over reads only the file in force and closes final from 16:15 ET (`tests/test_no_lookahead.py`). Tests: `tests/test_borrow_fees.py`.
- **IBKR borrow book** (`src/data/ibkr_borrow.py`): IBKR's public short-stock file (`ftp2.interactivebrokers.com`, user `shortstock`; shares available + annual fee for ~19,750 symbols) is downloaded in every tick's Step-1 pool and archived RAW in `data/ibkr_borrow/<ET date>/<HHMMSS>.txt.gz` (never delete — IBKR keeps no history); `borrow_at(ticker, when)` reads the file in force at any instant. Every NEW equity short is checked when opened (`enable_short_borrow_gate`): the selection short needs ≥ $10k available at any fee; the legacy entry paths also refuse a fee above `short_borrow_max_fee_pct` 50%/yr. No snapshot within 120 min → not checked (warned); futures / indices / FX / crypto are never judged. Tests: `tests/test_ibkr_borrow.py`.
- **Daily NAV** (`daily_nav.compute_compound_return`): a path-faithful daily walk over cached closes, capital-weighted per day and compounded; deterministic given the database.
- **Splits during a hold** (`tracker.apply_splits`, `enable_split_adjustment`; PREREG64, user 2026-10-09: "Fix the bugs in production"): from 04:00 ET of a split's execution date — the pre-market open; the overnight hours before it still quote the old terms — every OPEN trade is rescaled through the splits executed since its entry (the split data `intraday_store.split_rows`: Polygon lists announced splits ahead, refreshed nightly), as IBKR rescales the position: the price fields (`SPLIT_PRICE_FIELDS`: entry, reference close, fill, target, the pick's closes, the dip's close / trend / limit / ATR) x the factor, the share counts (`SPLIT_SHARE_FIELDS`: the accounts' shares, the broker's requested and filled quantities) / it — exact, a reverse split's fraction kept (IBKR pays it in cash; the broker's counts truncate); the dollars, the return and the cost fractions unchanged. Applied at every ledger load (`_load_trades`) and the broker's own (`reconcile.sync`), once (`split_factor`, the values before it in `split_orig`, `split_log`); the current mark carried from the terms of its own time; a fill IBKR reports later for an order submitted before the split (`reconcile._split_k`) and IBKR's borrow charges of earlier days (per share of THEIR day) come into the same terms; the squeeze cover reads the original entry times the factor (never twice). Without it a 1-for-10 reverse split read as a 900% loss on a short — a false margin call of the whole account — and the broker's share counts no longer matched IBKR's. Tests `tests/test_splits.py`.
- **Win rates are GROSS** (standing directive): a win is the right direction on raw prices (`tracker.gross_return_pct`, `is_gross_win`, `gross_win_rate`); costs live in the RETURN metrics only. Pinned by `tests/test_gross_winrate_convention.py`.
- **Self-calibrating parameters** share one idiom — prior → evidence → Bayesian shrinkage → clamps → fail-soft to the static setting — and report to the calibration registry (`src/performance/calibration.py` → the Data Quality tab).
- **Email performance** (`get_performance_for_email`): segment stats, the solo-method simulation and the method evaluation; `sel_short` trades carry no method scores, so they appear in no method row.

| **IBKR borrow book** (raw short-stock file) | the file's own ET timestamp | **permanent — never delete** | `data/ibkr_borrow/<date>/<HHMMSS>.txt.gz` |
| **IBKR Flex statements** (raw XML: the borrow fees IBKR charged) | fetch time | **permanent — never delete** | `data/ibkr_flex/` |
| Full news-article pool + its feeds | `url_hash` / (`url_hash`, `feed`) | `news_archive_retention_days` 730 | **DuckDB** `news_articles`, `news_article_feeds` |
| Trades · runs · recs · signals | — | permanent | **DuckDB** |

The sentiment verdict cache never delays a reaction to news: its key hashes the exact article set sent to the scorer. **A date-keyed cache also freezes the SHAPE of what it stores** — a new model field reaches old records only as those caches roll over. The DuckDB database is not a cache — never auto-expired.

### Adding a new data source

1. **Per-ticker signal:** implement a `[-1.0, +1.0]` scorer and an `enable_*` flag in `settings.py`. Add the method to `tracker._ALL_METHODS` **and** `schema.SIGNAL_BASE_METHOD_COLUMNS` (+ `_ADD_COLUMNS` for existing DBs) — `tests/test_db_signals.py` fails if they drift — plus `METHOD_CATEGORIES`, `METHOD_LABELS` and `METHOD_SOURCES`. An OHLCV scorer that should also run on 30-min / weekly goes in `multi_timeframe.TECHNICAL_METHODS`. New methods start **panel-first at weight 0** until the panel proves them; weighting one takes the four registrations above. **If you change what an existing scorer OUTPUTS, register the epoch.**
2. **Smart money signal:** return `List[InsiderTrade]` from a new module, call it from the Step 3 block, extend the `smart_money` list.
3. **Macro context:** return a Pydantic model and render it in the email template.
4. **News-like source:** return `List[NewsArticle]` (or pre-score via `provider_insights` to skip the LLM), submit it `_safe`-wrapped in the Step 1 fetch pool, add it to `_article_chunks`, add an `enable_*` flag. An always-on feed stays OUT of `data_quality.EXPECTED_SPARSE_SOURCES`; an optional / event-driven one goes IN.

### Broker integration

`src/broker/` is the real execution leg beside the internal ledger ("shadow & reconcile"). Modes `off | dry_run | ibkr_paper | ibkr_live` (`broker_mode`, live: `ibkr_paper`, port 4002; the factory refuses paper on a known live port). Interactive Brokers through `ib_async` (imported lazily) → IB Gateway under IBC (`docs/DEPLOYMENT.md`).

- **`reconcile.sync()`** runs TWICE per tick — in the live path right after the selection short's entries, and at the end of the tick (legacy exits, a late scorer's picks, a same-tick resend of anything the first left unfilled; the two reports are merged) — idempotent: **(0)** fill refresh by `orderRef`; **(0b)** tick-scoped order lifetime — unfilled orders are cancelled and their legs cleared; entries are resubmitted re-anchored through the price-aware gate (`broker_resubmit_max_adverse_bps` 100) EXCEPT the live books' entries (`sel_short`, `dip_long`), which are resent while their row is open; unfilled legacy entries are never sent while a live book is on (`LEGACY_ENTRY_SHADOWED`); exits are never gated; **(0c)** cancel-on-close; **(1)** entries size the GAP against any same-side holding NO OTHER open trade owns (`_owned_by_open`: the selection short stacks several open trades on one name, 2026-09-28); **(2)** closed trades still holding a position are closed from the actual position MINUS what open trades in the ticker own and MINUS the shares already being covered (the unfilled rest of working covers, covers sent earlier in the pass) — two closed trades on one name never cover it twice; a dead entry leg stays parked only when shares no other open trade owns back it; a closed trade whose own entry never filled sends nothing (`NEVER_FILLED`, terminal) and no position row means flat (no order) — never an exit sized from the recorded fill; an order IBKR REFUSES outright is resent in the same tick (`broker_refused_resends_per_tick`) and parked for the next (an entry is given up after `broker_refused_max_ticks`, an exit never); **(3)** drift → `broker_drift_action="flatten"` (never adopted into the ledger; `ibkr_live` always refuses auto-flatten); **(4)** settle — fill fast or kill (`broker_settle_seconds` 30, re-anchored every ~6 s).
- **Invariant**: no order works the book unless a ledger leg points at it — `_persist_legs` flushes after every submission, `cancel_order` cancels every order with a ref, a pre-submit duplicate guard, an orphan sweep, `reqAllOpenOrders()` on connect. Tests: `tests/test_broker_reliability.py`, `tests/test_broker_price_aware_resubmit.py`.
- **Reliability**: transient failures retry (`broker_submit_retries` 2 × 5 s); auto-reconnect (60 s cooldown); `IB.RequestTimeout` 45 s; the 600 s sync watchdog. Gateway auto-recovery (`broker_gateway_auto_restart`, paper only) is armed by the wedge detector: 5 consecutive request timeouts force a recycle, and 3 recycles within `broker_wedge_recycle_window_seconds` (3600) restart the gateway; `get_account()` (served from the local cache) never counts as an answer; the recycle history persists in `cache/broker_wedge_recycles.json`. A gateway with no API listener that is younger than `broker_gateway_boot_grace_seconds` (120; a relaunch opens its port ~25–45 s after start) is BOOTING — the recovery waits for its port instead of killing it, without consuming the cooldown (2026-09-29 03:02: a 22-s-old gateway was killed and relaunched); older and still no port = stuck → restarted. The relaunch (`gateway_recovery._relaunch`) waits for the killed gateway's `IBC Gateway` task instance to END before `schtasks /Run` — the task's IgnoreNew policy silently drops a run while StartGateway.bat is still closing (4 of 5 recoveries 2026-09-29..10-05 came back only at its 10-minute keep-alive) — ends one stuck past 20 s, and keeps a gateway the keep-alive already started. - **Orders**: a short entry under Rule 201 is offered one tick above the bid and rests until the next tick (above, `broker_ssr`); otherwise marketable `LMT` at the model price ± the session cap — `broker_limit_cap_bps` 45 in RTH (spread-aware up to 400 with a live book), 80 extended, 150 overnight; off-RTH forces LMT + `outsideRth`; class shares normalise `BRK-B` ↔ `BRK B`.
- **IBKR's statement** (`src/broker/flex.py`, user directive 2026-10-06): the EOD chain fetches, in its own thread (IBKR can take hours to generate a statement; one still generating is collected first on the next run, `data/ibkr_flex/pending.json`), the Activity Flex Query `ibkr_flex_query_id` with the Flex Web Service token `ibkr_flex_token` (both from `.env`; skipped while empty) — its Borrow Fees Details section is every fee IBKR charged the account, per short position per value date (quantity, price, value, rate, fee) → `broker_borrow_fees` (each statement period replaced whole, so a re-fetch never doubles a day) + the raw XML in `data/ibkr_flex/` (never delete). ## Standing rules

- **THE COMBINATION'S DESIGN** (user directive 2026-10-11: "the way to improve the combination model is to have different models (arms) that cover different part of the market and in different context. This way, a model can be more specific to trading short or long, and short term or long term. The idea is to add good quality models that have trades with a high return per day when they get activated because the market is favorable for them. We shouldn't activate them ourselves, they simply get more trades depending on the market conditions, like the dip buying model that's performing better and made more trades in set B. What matters is that whatever conditions the market is in the combination of the models produce good growth."): the live account improves by adding ARMS, each specific to a side (long / short) and a horizon (a quick trade / a swing / a long hold), whose OWN entry rule defines the market it trades in — never a regime switch we turn on or off (PREREG85: the bear-market effect on the shorts flipped sign between sets A and B).
